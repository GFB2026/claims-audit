## Methodology (appendix)

Every claim above was extracted from a public source (vendor site, press, review sites, regulatory filings) and independently checked by three separate reviewers instructed to *try to refute it* — a claim is marked "substantiated" only when at least two of three found real, independent corroboration beyond the vendor's own materials. This is the same standard used for adversarial fact-checking, not a single pass of "does this sound plausible."
