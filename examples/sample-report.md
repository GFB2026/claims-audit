# [Example] AI-Native Primary-Research Startup — Claims Audit

**Vendor evaluated:** an AI-native primary/expert-intelligence research platform, publicly announced seed funding in 2026 (identifying details redacted for this public example — this is a real run, on a real company, with the name removed)
**Scope:** 5 risk categories, 18 sources, 25 claims independently verified (3 adversarial votes each)

---

## Overall Risk: 🟡 MEDIUM

Leadership background, incorporation, and the funding round itself all corroborate independently. But the vendor's headline *scale and traction* numbers — the ones that would justify paying a premium or trusting the platform's data moat — could not be independently confirmed by anyone outside the company's own press release.

**Recommendation: PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS** — the company and its people are real and check out; treat every specific performance/scale metric as unverified until the vendor provides third-party-auditable evidence.

---

## Red Flags

- **"World's largest and fastest-growing [X] library"** (350,000+ minutes claimed) — no independent source corroborates this. A named competitor's own public scale figures, converted to the same unit, imply an order of magnitude *more* data than the claim — the superlative doesn't hold up to a same-unit comparison.
- **"30x revenue growth in 12 months," "200%+ net revenue retention," "100% customer renewal rate"** — appear only in the company's own funding announcement; no independent source (financial press, review site, customer reference) confirms any of the three.
- **"5-day turnaround at ~1/3 the cost of traditional research"** — the qualitative "faster/cheaper than traditional methods" framing holds up; the specific numbers attached to it do not have independent support.

## Confirmed Strengths

- **Leadership background** — the founder's prior senior research-industry role was independently corroborated across three separate professional-data sources, not just the company's own bio page.
- **Funding round amount and lead investors** — confirmed via the primary press release plus 8 independent syndicating outlets, all consistent, no contradicting figures found anywhere.
- **Company legal existence and officer roles** — independently confirmed via a public regulatory filing (not just a press release or the company's own site).

---

## Findings by Category

### Company Viability
**Verdict:** Substantiated
Incorporation, funding amount, and named leadership all independently confirmed via a regulatory filing search plus multiple non-syndicated press sources. A later, separate, larger raise (found via the same regulatory filing type) is consistent with — not contradictory to — the earlier round.

### Product & Technical Claims
**Verdict:** Partially substantiated
The *workflow description* (automated interview → analysis → delivery) is confirmed via the vendor's own product pages and is a plausible, coherent description of what the product does. The *scale claims* attached to that workflow (data volume, "world's largest") failed independent verification.

### Customer References
**Verdict:** No independent claims found
No third-party case study, named reference customer, or review-site presence was located independent of the vendor's own site at the time of this audit — this is itself worth noting to a buyer, not a confirmation either way.

---

## Methodology

Every claim above was extracted from a public source and checked by three independent reviewers instructed to *try to refute it first* — a claim is marked "substantiated" only when at least two of three found real, independent corroboration beyond the subject's own materials.

**Limits:** Public-claims audit only. Not a substitute for a signed security questionnaire, financial statement review, or reference calls — it tells a buyer where to focus those.
